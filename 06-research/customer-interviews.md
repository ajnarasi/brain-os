# Customer Interviews — Template + Synthesis

## Purpose

Validate (or kill) the Brain thesis by talking to real merchants before building anything. Goal: 3 mid-market fashion brands, 5 Clover SMB restaurants, 1 community bank channel partner.

## Interview template

### Part 1 — Their current reality (20 min)
1. Walk me through a typical week of payments ops at your business.
2. What's the hardest part of running payments that nobody outside your company would understand?
3. When something goes wrong (auth drop, dispute spike, settlement weirdness), how do you find out? Who do you call?
4. How much time per week does your team spend on payment ops tasks that feel like they should be automated?
5. Tell me about the last time you had a real launch or go-live event. What broke? How did you recover?

### Part 2 — Their memory of past pain (20 min)
6. What's a lesson you've learned about your own payment system that you had to learn the hard way?
7. When a team member leaves, what knowledge leaves with them? How much does that hurt?
8. How do you feel about the documentation your current processor / platform provides?

### Part 3 — Brain concept reaction (15 min)
*Walk through a 2-minute pitch. Not a demo — just the concept.*
9. What part of that would actually matter to you?
10. What part sounds like vaporware or marketing?
11. What would you need to see to believe it works?
12. If it worked, what would you pay for it monthly?

### Part 4 — Trust + adoption (10 min)
13. How much autonomy would you give an AI agent on your payment ops? Where's the line between "act" and "ask first"?
14. Would you trust this coming from [Fiserv / your bank / your POS vendor / a startup]? Does the source matter?
15. What would stop you from trying this today?

### Part 5 — Anything else (5 min)
16. What did I not ask that I should have?

## Synthesis template

After each interview, capture:
- **Key pain signal** (1–3 bullets)
- **Trust signal** (where they draw the "act" vs. "ask" line)
- **Willingness to pay** (specific dollar range)
- **Adoption blockers** (what would stop them)
- **Surprises** (anything that contradicted the thesis)

## After ~8–10 interviews, look for

- **Patterns** — do 60%+ of merchants name the same pain point?
- **Monetization ceiling** — what's the realistic price range?
- **Must-have features** — what's in the MVP vs. nice-to-have?
- **Thesis-killers** — did anyone say "I'd never use this because X"?

## Red flags that should stop the project

- "We don't have payments pain, we just pay the fees and move on" from >50% of interviewees
- "I'd never trust an AI with my payments" from >50% of interviewees
- "We already have [X] and it does this" pointing to a real competitor we missed
- "This is too complicated to be valuable" from >50%

## Green flags that should accelerate

- Specific war stories that map cleanly to a Brain capability
- Willingness to beta-test expressed without prompting
- Specific dollar figures >$0 volunteered when asked about pricing
- "When can I get this?" asked unprompted
