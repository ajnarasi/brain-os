# Strategic / Global

**GPV:** $5B+ | **Typical:** multi-region, multi-processor, public-company reporting, dedicated Fiserv relationship team | **Fiserv products:** custom everything

## JTBD
"Deploy a Brain inside my environment that only we use, with memory schemas we control."

## Primary pain
Data residency and regulatory complexity across regions, integration with their own ops platform, customized agent behavior, strict audit requirements.

## Brain posture
**White-glove embedded.** Brain runs inside the merchant's own environment (VPC, private cloud, or on-prem). Custom memory schemas. Negotiated SLAs. Potentially custom agents for their specific verticals.

## Monetization ceiling
Custom pricing — often 7-figure annual contracts. Long sales cycles (12–24 months).

## Data sources the Brain needs
Everything + customer's own systems + maybe other processors. Data integration is a meaningful part of the deal.

## Regulatory overlay
Everything. Multi-region data residency, vertical-specific regulatory, SOX, often country-specific compliance (GDPR, PSD2, DORA, etc.).

## Brain telemetry available
Negotiated per deal.

## Priority
V3+. Not even close to MVP. But worth keeping on the roadmap because (a) strategic-global deals are worth a disproportionate share of Brain revenue, (b) learnings from enterprise apply here, (c) some of Fiserv's biggest competitive battles are in this segment.
