# Mid-Market

**GPV:** $10M–$500M | **Typical:** 10–200 locations, light PM+IT team, growing multi-channel complexity | **Fiserv products:** CommerceHub Enterprise, Carat, Optis, multiple payment products

## JTBD
"Help my lean team punch above our weight on payments ops — and don't make us hire a payments PM."

## Primary pain
Integration complexity, multi-location analytics roll-up, BFCM / seasonal scaling, APM coverage across geographies, dispute volume growing faster than the team, optimization opportunities no one has time to chase.

## Brain posture
**Co-pilot with agency.** Agents run semi-autonomously inside a defined envelope. Humans approve anything financially material. Narratives delivered weekly and on-incident. This is the highest-ROI segment for the Brain in Year 1.

## Monetization ceiling
$499–$1,999/mo. Business tier. Willing to pay because the Brain is cheaper than hiring a second payments PM.

## Data sources the Brain needs
POS, OMS, e-com platform, ERP, inventory, settlement, dispute, fraud tool, TAM notes, sometimes CRM.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI, vertical-specific (HIPAA for healthcare, state licensing for some verticals).

## Brain telemetry available
Full on direct; near-full on ISV-embedded; restricted on bank-channel.

Mid-market fashion/apparel brands are the target of MVP Slice A. See `04-prd/mvp-scope.md`.
