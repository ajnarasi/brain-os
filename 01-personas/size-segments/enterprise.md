# Enterprise

**GPV:** $500M–$5B | **Typical:** national footprint, full PM/IT/procurement teams, often multi-processor | **Fiserv products:** Carat APIs, custom CommerceHub, multiple processing relationships

## JTBD
"Be a first-class primitive in my ops stack. Don't be another vendor I have to manage."

## Primary pain
Consolidating payments data across processors, proving optimization ROI to finance, integrating payments intel with their own BI and incident tooling.

## Brain posture
**API-first.** Brain is exposed as an API, not a UI — the merchant's own ops team calls it from their existing tools. Memory schema is customizable. Enterprise wants Brain as a building block, not a product.

## Monetization ceiling
$10K–$100K/mo depending on volume, complexity, custom agents. Negotiated per-customer.

## Data sources the Brain needs
Everything — their own ops tooling + all Fiserv data + probably other processors' data.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI always, HIPAA for healthcare, SOX for public-company merchants, vertical-specific.

## Brain telemetry available
Full (direct), but enterprise often has partial visibility to their own systems the Brain doesn't automatically have; data integration project is part of every enterprise deployment.

## Priority
V2. Not MVP. Enterprise sales cycles are 6–18 months; need the product to mature first. But enterprise is where the largest individual deal sizes live.
