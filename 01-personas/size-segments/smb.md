**GPV:** $1M–$10M | **Typical:** 1–10 locations, part-time ops person, owner is tech-adjacent | **Fiserv products:** Clover Pro, CommerceHub, ISV-embedded

## Primary pain
Daily close, dispute handling, fraud rule drift, staff turnover losing tribal knowledge, not enough time to look at analytics.

## Brain posture
**Do-it-for-me by default, escalate on ambiguity.** Agents draft and take action within a pre-approved envelope. Daily narratives, proactive incident alerts, dispute drafting.

## Monetization ceiling
$49–$99/mo/MID. Pro tier. Sweet spot for Clover app-market rev-share.

## Data sources the Brain needs
POS, e-com platform (if any), settlement, dispute, basic inventory.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI, possibly state-level (liquor for bars, etc.).

## Brain telemetry available
Full on direct + Clover; varies on third-party ISVs.
