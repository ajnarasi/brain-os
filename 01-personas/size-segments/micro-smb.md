# Micro-SMB

**GPV:** <$1M | **Typical:** owner-operator, single location, zero IT/PM capacity | **Fiserv products:** Clover Starter, basic CommerceHub

## JTBD
"Do as much of my payments ops for me as possible so I can run my business."

## Primary pain
No time, no technical capacity, no analytics literacy. Everything is a burden.

## Brain posture
**Zero-touch.** Proactive alerts. Plain-English. Agents act with minimal prompting (inside a conservative envelope). Free tier — this segment can't pay real SaaS prices but is hugely valuable as a flywheel for Clover App Market adoption.

## Monetization ceiling
$0–$10/mo. Loss leader. Monetization comes from keeping the merchant on Clover, not from Brain-direct revenue.

## Data sources the Brain needs
POS (Clover), settlement, dispute. Usually nothing else.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI only.

## Brain telemetry available
Full (Clover is Fiserv-owned).
