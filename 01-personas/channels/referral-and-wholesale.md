# Channel: Referral / Wholesale

Merchants who reach Fiserv through loose referral relationships — accountants, POS resellers, trade associations, chambers of commerce, buying groups.

- **Who owns the merchant:** Usually Fiserv direct (the referrer gets a kickback but doesn't own ongoing)
- **Who pays for Brain:** Merchant-direct
- **Data access:** Full (merchant is effectively a direct merchant)
- **Surface:** Fiserv-branded with optional "referred by X" attribution
- **Trust relationship:** Initial trust is with the referrer, then transfers to Fiserv
- **Typical size:** SMB
- **Typical pain:** Similar to direct SMB merchants
- **Brain autonomy default:** Same as direct

## Brain experience
- Essentially identical to the direct channel experience, with a tracking attribution to the referrer
- Referrers may see an aggregate view of their referred merchants for retention / upsell purposes

## Notes
Lowest-priority channel for Brain-specific work because it's not materially different from direct. Include it in the segmentation doc for completeness, but don't build channel-specific agents.
