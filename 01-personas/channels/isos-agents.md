# Channel: ISOs / Agent Networks

Independent Sales Organizations (ISOs) and agent networks who resell Fiserv acquiring in exchange for residuals. The classic "feet on the street" channel that predates direct sales by decades.

- **Who owns the merchant:** The ISO (who owns the residual relationship)
- **Who pays for Brain:** ISO (rev-share) or merchant-direct
- **Data access:** Varies by ISO agreement; often txn-only, limited dispute/settlement access
- **Surface:** Co-branded or ISO-branded
- **Trust relationship:** Merchant trusts the ISO/agent who signed them
- **Typical size:** Micro-SMB, SMB
- **Typical pain:** ISOs are retention-squeezed; merchants churn to competitor ISOs for small rate savings
- **Brain autonomy default:** Medium

## Brain experience
- Brain is positioned to the ISO as a retention tool: "you sell this to your merchants, they stay longer, your residuals grow."
- ISO dashboards show Brain-adoption metrics across their book
- Co-branded surface (ISO logo + Fiserv-powered attribution)
- Escalation path: Brain → ISO agent → Fiserv

## Why this channel matters
- ISOs control a large slice of SMB acquiring
- ISOs have zero differentiation today besides price — Brain is a real differentiator
- Rev-share can be structured so ISOs absorb the cost

## Risks
- ISOs are numerous and fragmented — high sales cost to land each one
- Data agreements vary wildly — Brain capabilities must be configurable per ISO contract
- Some ISOs are low-sophistication and will resist a technical product
