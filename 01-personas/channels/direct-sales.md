# Channel: Direct Sales

Merchants who signed directly with Fiserv through field or inside sales — CommerceHub Enterprise, Optis, First Data direct, Carat API customers.

- **Who owns the merchant:** Fiserv
- **Who pays for Brain:** Merchant (or bundled into their Fiserv contract)
- **Data access:** Full (txn, dispute, settlement, TAM notes, KB)
- **Surface:** Fiserv-branded merchant portal
- **Trust relationship:** Direct with Fiserv
- **Typical size:** Mid-market to strategic global
- **Typical pain:** Complex integration, cross-product ops, enterprise-grade compliance
- **Brain autonomy default:** Higher (merchant can grant agent action permissions quickly)

## Brain experience
- Full catalog of agents available
- Integration phase is white-glove + Brain-accelerated
- Operate phase includes direct API access to Brain memory for the merchant's own tooling
- Escalation path: Fiserv TAM → Fiserv solutions engineer → product team

## Notes
Direct is the "easy" channel for the Brain because Fiserv owns everything. It's also the smallest channel by merchant count (though large by GPV). Start here to prove the product, then port to harder channels.
