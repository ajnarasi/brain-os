# Vertical: Government & Public

Sub-verticals: municipal services, utility, DMV, court fees, tax payments, public transit, parks & recreation.

## Characteristic pain
Level-3 interchange data, convenience fee arithmetic, public records / audit trail requirements, long procurement cycles, strict uptime requirements.

## Brain-relevant data sources
Government ERPs, utility billing, transit fare systems.

## Regulatory overlay
Level-3 data rules, state-specific convenience fee rules, public records requirements, strict audit logging.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **Level-3 enrichment:** get the lowest interchange
- **Convenience-fee arithmetic:** state-specific calculation rules
- **Public-audit trail:** every Brain action exportable for public records requests

## MVP fit
Not in v1. Long sales cycles. V3+.

## Monetization ceiling
Enterprise / custom. Often tied into broader Fiserv government-services deals.
