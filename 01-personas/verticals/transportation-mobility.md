# Vertical: Transportation & Mobility

Sub-verticals: rideshare, last-mile delivery, freight/trucking, parking, toll, EV charging.

## Characteristic pain
Incremental auth (hold on ride start, capture on end), multi-party payout (driver, platform, tax), real-time disbursement, EV charging settlement, fleet/fuel card acceptance.

## Brain-relevant data sources
Dispatch systems, telematics, EV charging network APIs, fleet card interchange tables.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI, incremental auth rules, real-time disbursement regs (Push-to-card), fleet card rules.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **Incremental auth state:** mid-ride hold / release / capture orchestration
- **Driver payout orchestration:** split, time, tax, instant-pay options
- **EV charging settlement:** the charging network is a new payment category with new quirks

## MVP fit
Not in v1. Interesting for Fiserv's mobility partnerships but not a priority until the platform-channel work matures.

## Monetization ceiling
Mid-market to enterprise. Mobility platforms are usually marketplaces — also relevant to the marketplace channel.
