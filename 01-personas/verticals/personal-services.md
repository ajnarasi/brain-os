# Vertical: Personal Services

Sub-verticals: salons/spas, barber shops, fitness/gyms, yoga/pilates, pet services, auto repair/detailing, home services (HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, cleaning), dry cleaning.

## Characteristic pain
Gratuity handling, booking integration, membership dunning, staff turnover, recurring billing reliability, mobile/on-site POS.

## Brain-relevant data sources
Booking system (Mindbody, Square Appointments, Clover), membership CRM, mobile POS.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI, state-level for certain services.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **Gratuity split:** tip distribution rules
- **Booking integration:** link payment to appointment state
- **Membership dunning:** recurring billing recovery tuned for fitness/spa churn patterns
- **"Staff leaves, brain remembers":** operational continuity through turnover

## MVP fit
Not in v1. Part of the Year 2 SMB expansion via Clover App Market.

## Monetization ceiling
Micro to SMB. Volume play through Clover.
