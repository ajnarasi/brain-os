# Vertical: Education

Sub-verticals: K-12 schools, colleges & universities, childcare, tutoring, EdTech subscription platforms.

## Characteristic pain
Tuition payment plans, convenience fee rules, FERPA constraints, PCI + FERPA intersection, student vs. parent payer workflows, convenience-fee arithmetic, multi-institution reconciliation.

## Brain-relevant data sources
SIS (student information system), LMS, tuition management, billing, settlement.

## Regulatory overlay
**FERPA** — student privacy. PCI. Convenience fee rules (state-specific).

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **Tuition plan dunning:** tuned for long-tail education payment plans
- **Convenience fee calc:** state-specific rules
- **Student-payer workflows:** parent, student, grandparent, employer-sponsored, scholarship

## MVP fit
Not in v1. V3+.

## Monetization ceiling
Mid-market to enterprise (universities). SMB for private K-12 and childcare.
