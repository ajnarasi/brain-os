# Vertical: Digital & Subscription

Sub-verticals: SaaS, streaming/media, gaming (where allowed), digital goods, ad tech, subscription boxes.

## Characteristic pain
Dunning optimization, account updater orchestration, churn analytics, global tax, App Store reconciliation, cross-border acceptance, card brand rules for recurring.

## Brain-relevant data sources
Subscription billing (Stripe Billing, Chargebee, Zuora, Recurly), account updater feed, churn events, global tax provider.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI, global tax (VAT, GST, sales tax), card-brand recurring rules, App Store / Play Store rules for in-app subs.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **Dunning optimization:** retry logic tuned to decline code + issuer patterns
- **Account updater orchestration:** automatic card refresh via network account updater
- **Churn narrative:** "here's why customers are leaving this month"
- **MRR analytics narratives:** standard SaaS metrics as plain-English stories

## MVP fit
Not in v1. Subscription merchants are usually Stripe-native, so Fiserv has less footprint here. Year 2+ consideration if Fiserv's subscription product footprint grows.

## Monetization ceiling
Mid-market to enterprise. Subscription businesses are sophisticated buyers and will pay for dunning lift.
