# Vertical: Financial & Regulated

Sub-verticals: lenders / non-bank financial institutions, debt collection, money service businesses, insurance premium finance, iGaming (licensed).

## Characteristic pain
KYC/AML, state licensing, NMLS compliance, strict chargeback rules, high-risk underwriting, FinCEN MSB registration.

## Brain-relevant data sources
Core banking, lending LOS, KYC providers (Alloy, Persona, Onfido), AML monitoring, state license tracking.

## Regulatory overlay
Heavy. KYC/AML, state-by-state licensing, NMLS, FinCEN MSB, card-brand high-risk MCC rules.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **KYC orchestration:** chain of KYC providers with fallback logic
- **State-license state tracking:** alerts for expirations, new state rollouts
- **NMLS compliance:** regulatory reporting support

## MVP fit
Not in v1. Regulatory surface is too complex for an MVP. V3+, and only for licensed merchants (nothing outside regulatory scope).

## Monetization ceiling
Enterprise. Willing to pay heavily for compliance leverage.
