# Vertical: Professional Services

Sub-verticals: legal, accounting, consulting, real estate, property management, insurance, staffing, marketing/creative agencies.

## Characteristic pain
Trust account handling (attorneys), retainer dunning, surcharging rules, Level-2 card data for B2B, tax-deduction documentation, client-billing integration.

## Brain-relevant data sources
Practice management system, CRM, billing system, trust account ledger, Level-2 interchange tables.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI, state bar rules for trust accounts, surcharging state-by-state, AML for real estate.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **Trust account reconciliation:** critical for attorneys; mistakes are disbarment-level
- **Retainer dunning:** tuned for professional billing
- **Level-2 data enrichment:** lower interchange cost on B2B transactions
- **Surcharging compliance:** state-level rules on what can be surcharged

## MVP fit
Not in v1. Interesting V2+ target, especially for mid-market law firms and accounting practices where Brain ROI is high.

## Monetization ceiling
SMB to mid-market. Willing to pay because the alternative is hiring a bookkeeper.
