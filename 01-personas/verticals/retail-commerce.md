# Vertical: Retail & Commerce

Sub-verticals: general retail, fashion/apparel, grocery, c-store+fuel, electronics, home goods, beauty/cosmetics, luxury, jewelry, sporting goods.

## Characteristic pain
Inventory-aware fraud, returns, BFCM scaling, omnichannel reconciliation, APM coverage per geography, chargebacks from "item not received," BOPIS complexity.

## Brain-relevant data sources
POS, e-com platform, OMS, inventory system, returns, fraud tool, settlement, dispute.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI only for most; cross-border retail adds tax/VAT complexity.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **Inventory-aware fraud:** correlate fraud signals with inventory velocity to avoid blocking legitimate rush on hot SKUs
- **Return narratives:** why return rates are where they are, which SKUs drive them, which customers are problems
- **BFCM brain:** launch brain tuned for seasonal scaling
- **APM-per-geo guidance:** leverages APM Checkout SDK for cross-border APM rollout
- **BOPIS reconciliation:** for omnichannel merchants

## MVP fit
**Slice A beachhead.** Mid-market fashion/apparel brands (sub-segment of this cluster) are the value pilot. See `04-prd/mvp-scope.md`.

## Monetization ceiling
Mid-market: $499–$1,999/mo. Enterprise: $10K+/mo. Very willing to pay for conversion lift because they live and die on it.
