# Vertical: Nonprofit & Faith

Sub-verticals: nonprofits, charities, churches, religious organizations, political campaigns.

## Characteristic pain
Donor privacy, recurring giving reliability, giving-day operational spikes, campaign finance rules, 501(c) reporting, donor stewardship.

## Brain-relevant data sources
Donor CRM (Blackbaud, Raiser's Edge, Salesforce NPSP), giving platform, settlement.

## Regulatory overlay
Donor privacy, 501(c) reporting, FEC rules for political campaigns, state-level charity registration.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **Recurring giving dunning:** tuned for donor psychology (different from commercial churn)
- **Giving-day ops brain:** launch brain for high-concentration events (Giving Tuesday, GivingDay campaigns)
- **Campaign-cycle analytics:** tied to fundraising cycles
- **Donor stewardship:** narrative summaries for the dev team

## MVP fit
Not in v1. V3+.

## Monetization ceiling
SMB to mid-market. Nonprofits are price-sensitive but stable customers.
