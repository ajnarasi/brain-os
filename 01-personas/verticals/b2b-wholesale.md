# Vertical: B2B & Wholesale

Sub-verticals: distributors, manufacturers, industrial supply, trade supply, agriculture, fleet/fuel, construction supply.

## Characteristic pain
Level 2/3 card data (big interchange savings), purchasing card acceptance, NET terms + ACH, EDI integration, AR reconciliation, check conversion, large-ticket transactions.

## Brain-relevant data sources
ERP (SAP, NetSuite, Dynamics, JD Edwards), AR system, EDI translator, purchasing card interchange tables.

## Regulatory overlay
PCI, Level 2/3 data rules, NACHA for ACH, export controls for certain industries.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **Level 2/3 enrichment:** automated population of line-item data to lower interchange
- **NET terms dunning:** tuned for B2B collection (longer cycles, bigger balances)
- **Purchasing card acceptance:** handle P-card specifics
- **ACH orchestration:** pair card and ACH intelligently

## MVP fit
Not in v1. Very attractive V2 target because Level 2/3 optimization alone can pay for the Brain multiple times over for mid-market B2B.

## Monetization ceiling
Mid-market to enterprise. Strong ROI story on interchange savings.
