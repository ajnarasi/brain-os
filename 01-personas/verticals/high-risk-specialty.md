# Vertical: High-Risk / Specialty

Sub-verticals: cannabis/CBD (where allowed), firearms retail, nutraceuticals, adult content, travel aggregators, subscription clubs, lead-gen, debt consolidation, MLM.

## Characteristic pain
MATCH list exposure, elevated reserves, limited card-brand access (Visa/MC restrict some MCCs), alternative rail dependency (ACH, crypto for cannabis), state-specific legality, reputation risk.

## Brain-relevant data sources
Alternative payment rails, state legal compliance trackers, MATCH list exposure monitoring.

## Regulatory overlay
Depends on vertical — cannabis is state-by-state legal, firearms has federal + state rules, adult has card-brand restrictions, nutraceuticals has FDA adjacent rules.

## Vertical-specific Brain capabilities
- **High-risk reserve tracking:** monitor reserve balances against card-brand requirements
- **Alternative-rail routing:** when card payment isn't viable
- **MATCH-risk monitoring:** avoid getting merchants on the MATCH list
- **State-legality tracking:** especially critical for cannabis

## MVP fit
Not in v1. Ever, possibly. High-risk is deliberately a narrow, cautious segment for Fiserv and probably should stay that way. The Brain could help here but the regulatory downside of a mistake is asymmetric.

## Monetization ceiling
Varies. Some high-risk verticals pay a premium because service is scarce; others can't because margins are compressed.
