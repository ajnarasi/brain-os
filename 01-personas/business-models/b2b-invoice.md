# Business Model: B2B Invoice-Based

Selling to other businesses. NET terms, ACH, Level 2/3 card data, purchasing cards, AR integration.

## Characteristic pattern
- Lower transaction count, higher ticket size
- NET-30 / NET-60 / NET-90 payment terms
- Level 2/3 data = significant interchange savings
- ACH alongside card
- AR system integration

## Brain-relevant data sources
ERP (SAP, NetSuite, Dynamics), AR system, purchasing card interchange tables, EDI

## Memory schema
Merchant → Customer-Business → Invoice → Payment → PO-reference

## Brain capabilities that matter most
- Level 2/3 data enrichment (massive interchange savings)
- NET terms dunning
- Interchange optimization
- ACH/card routing

## Typical size
Mid-market to enterprise. SMB B2B exists but doesn't use Brain-class sophistication.
